# Mallard Duck (Drake), Stylized, Rigged & Animated (v3), by Liray 3D

Source: *Duck_Stylized_GLB.zip* ("Cozy Pond - Freshwater Friends" series), supplied by the project owner.
Licence: **Standard Fab licence** (as stated in the pack's README.txt). Made by Liray 3D. The pack's README notes it
was made with AI tools under Liray 3D's creative direction and reviewed by hand.

Used as `assets/animals/duck.glb` (scripts/gfx/duck.gd). Modified for the game: from the pack's LOD1 GLB, the face
shape keys and their morph animation tracks were removed, the mesh decimated from 75,758 to about 5,000 triangles,
the normal maps dropped and the colour textures scaled down (body 512, parts 256, eye 128); the rig and its twelve
clips (Idle, Walk, Run, Swim, Eat, Quack, Flap, Blink, Emote_Happy / Sad / Surprised / Angry) are kept. The original
download is unchanged outside the repository.
