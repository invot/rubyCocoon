# Window design references

Inspected from the user-supplied GLB files on 2026-11-09. Creator, source and license information below comes from
each file's `asset.extras` metadata.

- **Stylized Cartoon Windows** by [Canino3d](https://sketchfab.com/Canino3d),
  [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylized-cartoon-windows-22f58cb5690744e39052bb5c1fd52267),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Supplied as `stylized_cartoon_windows.glb`.
- **Windows Pack** by [Midox](https://sketchfab.com/ahmedhamm8),
  [source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/windows-pack-4321eaf0a32d47ec91ff17a6fa9a0e44),
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Supplied as `windows_pack.glb`.

These packs were rendered and used as visual references for exaggerated surrounds, caps, sills, mullions and
masonry arches. The game's replacement geometry and vertex colours are generated by `scripts/gfx/cartoon_windows.gd`;
no source meshes or textures are included in the game. Original downloads remain untouched. Temporary input copies
and preview renders are under the ignored `build/` directory.
