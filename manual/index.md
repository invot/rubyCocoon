# Ruby Cocoon manual

[◀ README](../README.md)

The [README](../README.md) covers running the game, system requirements and controls. These pages go deeper: the walkthrough follows the journey map by map, the mechanics page explains the rules behind it, and the rest are reference pages for the interface, art, settings, phones and development.

Each page links back here. The walkthrough also has previous and next map links at the end of every map, and the long pages begin with a contents list.

## Start here

| Page | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Walkthrough](walkthrough.md) | The journey in play order, map by map: what happens, who to talk to, and what opens next. |
| [Gameplay mechanics](gameplay.md) | Beasts, eggs, growth, moves and elements, the battle ring, weapons, the flute, shops and saving. |
| [Progression and unlocks](progression.md) | One ordered table of story objectives, the gate that holds each, and what it opens, with demo-only steps marked. |
